Getting Set Up

Set up your professional Email

A branded inbox at your own domain — you@yourbusiness.com — instantly makes you look more established and trustworthy. How you set it up depends on what you already have, so pick your situation below.

✉️ Email · Professional Inbox
Tip: use Print → Save as PDF to keep or share this guide.

Why we need this

A domain email builds instant credibility. "info@yourbusiness.com" reads as far more professional than a personal Gmail or Yahoo address — and it keeps your business communication in one place.

You'll create the mailbox (it's tied to your identity and billing), and we handle the technical connection — the behind-the-scenes DNS setup that makes it work with your domain. You never share your password.

Step 1 · Pick your setup

★ Recommended
Google Workspace
Best for most businesses. Gmail-powered, reliable, easy to use. ~$6–7/user per month.
Alternative
Microsoft 365
Great if you already use Outlook, Word, or Excel. Email + Office apps. ~$6+/user per month.
Simplest
Email from your domain host
Basic inbox often included with your domain (GoDaddy, etc.). Fewer features, lowest cost.
🤝Not sure which fits? Just tell us and we'll recommend one — we can set most of it up with you on a quick call.
Option A

Google Workspace

1

Start signup

Go to workspace.google.com and click Get started. Enter your business name, rough number of people, and country.

2

Enter your domain

Type your website domain (yourbusiness.com) — the same one we're building your site on.

3

Create your mailbox

Choose your address, like info@yourbusiness.com or yourname@yourbusiness.com, and set a password. Pay for the plan (this is billed to you).

4

Hand the "verification" step to us

Google will show a verification code or "DNS record" to prove you own the domain. Don't worry about this part — copy it and send it to us, and we'll add it for you.

📧Send the code/records to tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us. Optionally, add us as an admin (Admin console → Users) if you'd like us to configure it fully.
Option B

Microsoft 365

1

Choose a plan

Go to microsoft365.com → For business. Business Basic covers professional email; higher tiers add the Office apps.

2

Enter your business & domain

Fill in your business details and your domain (yourbusiness.com).

3

Create your mailbox

Set up your address (you@yourbusiness.com) and password, and complete checkout (billed to you).

4

Send us the domain records

Microsoft will list DNS records to connect your domain. Copy them and send them over — we'll add them for you so email starts flowing.

📧Send the records to tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us. Add us as an admin if you'd like us to finish the config.
Option C

Email included with your domain host

1

Log into your domain host

Sign in wherever you registered your domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, etc.).

2

Find the Email section

Look for Email or Professional Email in your dashboard. Many hosts include one free or low-cost mailbox.

3

Create your mailbox

Create you@yourbusiness.com, set a password, and save. Because it's on the same host as your domain, it usually connects automatically.

💡Basic host email is simpler with fewer features — perfectly fine for a single professional inbox. Let us know if anything needs connecting and we'll handle the DNS.
🚫Whichever you choose: never send us your email password. We only ever need the verification code/DNS records, or to be added as an admin — not your login.

✔ What to send us when you're done

  • Which option you picked (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or host email).
  • The email address you created (e.g. info@yourbusiness.com).
  • Any verification code or DNS records the provider gave you — paste them to us and we'll connect it.
