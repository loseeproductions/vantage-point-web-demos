Getting Set Up

Set up Calendly for online booking

A simple, step-by-step guide to creating your Calendly account so customers can book appointments right from your website — and every booking lands on your calendar automatically.

📅 Calendly · Bookings
Tip: use Print → Save as PDF to keep or share this guide.
⏱️ About 10 minutes📄 Have ready: the calendar you already use (Google, Outlook, etc.)

Why we need this

Calendly turns your website into a booking machine. Customers pick an open time, and the appointment drops straight onto your calendar — no phone tag, no double-bookings.

It needs to connect to your calendar to know when you're free, so you'll create the account. Then you simply send us your booking link and we'll embed it beautifully into your site. You never share your password.

Step-by-step

1

Create your account

Go to calendly.com and click Sign up. If you use Gmail, click Sign up with Google — it's the fastest and connects your calendar in one step.

💡The free plan is plenty to start. You can always upgrade later.
2

Connect your calendar

Choose the calendar you already use — Google, Outlook/Microsoft, or iCloud — and sign in to allow access. This lets Calendly show your real availability and add new bookings automatically.

✅Calendly only reads when you're busy vs. free — it doesn't share your event details with anyone.
3

Set your availability

Tell Calendly the days and hours you're open to appointments (e.g. Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm). Customers will only be able to book inside those windows.

4

Create an appointment type

Click Create → Event Type. Give it a clear name like "Free Consultation — 30 min," set the length, and choose the location (phone call, in-person, or video).

5

Add video (optional)

If you meet virtually, connect Zoom or Google Meet under the event's location settings — Calendly will auto-create a meeting link for every booking.

6

Copy your booking link for us

This is all we need to add Calendly to your website. On your event, click Copy link — it looks like calendly.com/your-name/consultation.

🔗Send that link to tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us and we'll embed it on your site.
🚫No need to share your password. If you're on a paid plan and want us to fine-tune settings for you, you can instead invite tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us as a team member.

✔ What to send us when you're done

  • Your Calendly booking link (e.g. calendly.com/your-name/…).
  • Which calendar you connected, so we can confirm bookings flow through.
  • The name(s) of the appointment type(s) you'd like on the site.
Questions? Email tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us — we'll walk you through anything that's unclear.
