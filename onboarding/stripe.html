Getting Set Up

Connect Stripe to take payments

A simple, step-by-step guide to creating your Stripe account so your new website can accept credit card payments. No tech experience needed — just follow along.

💳 Stripe · Payments
Tip: use Print → Save as PDF to keep or share this guide.
⏱️ About 15 minutes 📄 Have ready: your business info & bank account

Why we need this

Stripe is how your website collects money. When a customer pays or books online, Stripe processes the card and deposits the funds straight into your bank account.

Because it handles real money, Stripe legally requires the account to be created by you, the business owner — we can't do this part for you. The good news: it only takes a few minutes, and once it's done you'll simply add us to your team so we can build your checkout. You never share your password or bank details with us.

Step-by-step

1

Create your account

Go to stripe.com and click Sign up (top-right). Enter your email, your full name, and a password you'll remember.

✅This account belongs to you and your business. You stay in full control the entire time.
2

Confirm your email

Stripe sends a confirmation email. Open your inbox, find the message from Stripe, and click Verify email address. (Check spam if you don't see it within a minute.)

3

Tell Stripe about your business

Stripe will ask a few questions to activate payments. Enter your business name, address, business type (e.g. Sole Proprietor or LLC), and a short description of what you do. For website, use the address of your new site.

💡Not sure about "business type"? If it's just you and you haven't formed an LLC, choose Individual / Sole Proprietor. You can change this later.
4

Verify your identity

By law, Stripe must confirm who you are. You'll enter your legal name, date of birth, home address, and the last 4 digits of your SSN (or your business EIN). This is standard and secure.

🔒This information goes directly to Stripe only. Vantage Point never sees it.
5

Add your bank account

Enter the routing number and account number for the bank account where you want your money deposited. This is where every payment will land.

6

Turn on extra security (recommended)

Stripe may offer two-step verification (a code sent to your phone when you log in). We strongly recommend turning this on to keep your account safe.

7

Invite Vantage Point to your team

This is the important one — it lets us build your checkout without ever seeing your password or bank info.

In Stripe, go to Settings → Team and security → Team, then click + New member. Enter our email and choose a limited role:

📧Invite tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us · Role: Developer (or Analyst)

Click Send invite and you're done — we'll accept it on our end.

🚫Never send us your password, your bank details, or your "secret API keys." You don't need to — inviting us to your team is all we require.

  • The business name exactly as you'd like it to appear on customer receipts.
  • Anything you got stuck on — we're happy to hop on a quick call.
