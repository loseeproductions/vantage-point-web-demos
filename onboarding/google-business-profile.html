Getting Set Up

Claim your Google Business Profile

A simple, step-by-step guide to setting up the listing that shows your business on Google Search & Maps — reviews, hours, photos, and a call button. It's one of the biggest drivers of local customers, and we'll optimize it for you.

📍 Google Business Profile · Local Search
Tip: use Print → Save as PDF to keep or share this guide.
⏱️ About 15 minutes (+ a few days to verify)📄 Have ready: a Google account & your business details

Why we need this

Your Google Business Profile is how local customers find you. When someone Googles your type of business, this listing is what shows up on the map with your reviews, hours, photos, and a click-to-call button.

Google requires the real business owner to claim and verify it — that's you. Once it's verified, you add us as a Manager and we'll optimize the whole thing (categories, photos, posts) to pull in more calls and bookings. You always stay the owner.

Step-by-step

1

Go to Google Business Profile

Visit google.com/business and click Manage now. Sign in with your Google account (or create a free one if you don't have one).

2

Find or add your business

Type your business name. If Google already lists it, select it to claim it. If it's not there, choose Add your business to Google.

💡If someone else appears to have claimed it, don't worry — Google has a "Request access" process. Let us know and we'll guide you.
3

Enter your business details

Fill in your category (what you do), address (or service area if you go to customers), phone number, and website — use your new site's address.

4

Verify that it's really you

Google confirms you own the business — usually by postcard, phone, email, or a short video. Follow whichever option it offers and complete it.

⏳Verification by postcard can take 5–14 days, so start this early. Everything else can move forward while you wait.
5

Add hours, photos & a description

Once verified, add your business hours, a few photos, and a short description. Don't stress about perfection here — this is exactly what we'll polish for you.

6

Add Vantage Point as a Manager

This lets us optimize your listing without ever owning it or needing your password.

In your Business Profile, open Settings → People and access (sometimes "Business Profile managers"), click Add, and enter our email with the Manager role:

📧Invite tim@vantagepointwebdesign.us · Role: Manager
✅You remain the Owner the whole time. A Manager can edit and post, but can't remove you or take ownership.
