Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026  ·  Vantage Point Web Design, a DBA of Losee Productions LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit vantagepointwebdesign.us (the "Site") or use our web design and digital product services (the "Services"). It also describes your rights and choices regarding your personal information under CalOPPA, CCPA/CPRA, and GDPR.

1. Who We Are

Vantage Point Web Design is a DBA of Losee Productions LLC, a California-based company providing web design and digital product services.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide directly

  • Contact form submissions: name, email, phone, business name, message content
  • Scheduling information via Cal.com (name, email, appointment details)
  • Client and billing information when you engage our Services
  • Payment information processed by Stripe (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Email marketing sign-up: email address and name

B. Information collected automatically

  • Analytics and usage data via Google Analytics (active) and Meta Pixel (not currently active, reserved for future use)
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 6)

C. Information from third parties

We may receive information from service providers including Resend, Cal.com, Google Analytics, and Meta Pixel in connection with the services they provide to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our Services, schedule appointments, process payments, send communications, analyze Site usage, detect fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

GDPR legal bases: performance of a contract, consent (marketing/non-essential cookies), legitimate interests, and legal obligation.

3A. Customer Information We Handle For Our Clients

Separately from information about visitors to this Site, our services involve handling customer records that belong to our business clients, such as their customer lists, quote and job records, and lead source information.

For that information we act as a service provider, not as the owner. We use it only to perform the services the client hired us for. We do not sell it, share it, combine it with any other party's data, or use it for our own purposes. The client can obtain a copy or require deletion at any time.

If you are a customer of one of our clients and want to know what is held about you, or want it corrected or deleted, please contact that business directly. They own the record. We will support them in responding.

The full terms are set out in our Data Processing Addendum.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with service providers (Stripe, Resend, Cal.com, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel), for legal and safety purposes, and in connection with business transfers.

5. Categories of Information Disclosed (CCPA/CPRA)

In the preceding 12 months we have collected and disclosed to service providers: identifiers, customer records, internet/network activity, and commercial information. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

6. Cookie Policy

We use strictly necessary cookies for Site operation. With your consent we also use Google Analytics to count visits and record where a visit came from. Until you choose Accept All in the cookie banner, Google Analytics runs in a mode that stores no cookies. We do not currently run Google Ads or Meta Ads; if that changes we will update this page first. You can also control cookies in your browser. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out request.

7. Email Marketing Consent

We send marketing emails only to individuals who have opted in, in compliance with CAN-SPAM and GDPR. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Opt out at any time by emailing info@vantagepointwebdesign.us. Email managed through Resend.

8. Your Privacy Rights

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know, access, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, limit use of sensitive personal information, and non-discrimination.

EU/UK residents (GDPR): You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and to withdraw consent. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise rights: info@vantagepointwebdesign.us or (714) 470-3891.

9. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain analytics/advertising cookies may constitute "sharing" under CCPA/CPRA. To opt out: email us with subject "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information," enable GPC in your browser, or use our cookie consent banner.

10. Data Retention

Client and contract records: 7 years. Analytics data: 24 months. We delete or de-identify data after these periods unless a longer period is required by law.

11. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us at info@vantagepointwebdesign.us if you believe a child has submitted information.

12. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

13. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where GDPR applies, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. The "Effective Date" above will reflect the most recent revision.
