About Vantage Point

Vantage Point Web Design is a small, independent web-design studio, a DBA of Losee Productions LLC, that builds custom websites and local SEO for small local service businesses across the United States. Our whole model is built on removing risk for the owner: every account stays in your name, every price is published, and the work starts with a free look at your own records rather than a sales call.

Why we start with your own numbers

Most agencies ask a small business for a deposit before showing anything, and plenty of owners have been burned paying upfront for work that never delivered. We start the other way round. Send an export of your customer list and we will tell you what is actually in it, at no charge, before any money changes hands. Usually that includes how many of your leads arrived with no source recorded at all. If those numbers are not worth anything to you, we stop there and it cost you an email.

Who we serve

We focus on the businesses that need a strong web presence the most but have the least time to build one: plumbers, HVAC companies, electricians, roofers, landscapers, salons and barbershops, restaurants, and other local service businesses. We speak plain English, not tech jargon, and we handle the whole thing, design, hosting, domain and email, Google Business Profile, and local SEO, so you can stay focused on running your business.

How we work

  • You send one export of your customer list and quotes. No cost, no logins, no commitment.
  • We tell you the three biggest things in it, free.
  • If it is worth going further, you approve the exact price before any payment. Every price is published on the site.
  • We rebuild the site so every lead arrives with its source attached, and fix what is broken in the records.
  • Each month we keep the tracking working and hand you three decisions with a dollar on each.

The company

Vantage Point Web Design operates as a DBA of Losee Productions LLC, a United States company. We work remotely and serve local businesses nationwide, building and supporting your site online.
