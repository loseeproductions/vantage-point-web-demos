About Vantage Point

Vantage Point Web Design is a small, independent web-design studio that builds custom websites and local SEO for small local service businesses across the United States. Our whole model is built on removing risk for the owner: every account stays in your name, every price is published, and the work starts with a scorecard you can check yourself rather than a sales call.

Get your scorecard See pricing

Why we start with a scorecard

Before any call, we look at your business the way a customer would and write down what we find: two pages, up to ten findings, each with a check you can run yourself. If the findings are not worth anything to you, we stop there.

Who we serve

We focus on the businesses that need a strong web presence the most but have the least time to build one: plumbers, HVAC companies, electricians, roofers, landscapers, salons and barbershops, restaurants, and other local service businesses. We speak plain English, not tech jargon, and we handle the whole thing, design, hosting, domain and email, Google Business Profile, and local SEO, so you can stay focused on running your business.

How we work

  • We send you a two-page scorecard built from public information. No logins, no export, no commitment.
  • You run the checks yourself and decide whether it is worth a conversation.
  • If it is worth going further, you approve the exact price before any payment. Every price is published on the site.
  • We rebuild the site so every lead arrives with its source attached, and fix what is broken in the records.
  • Each month we keep the tracking working and hand you three decisions with a dollar on each.

The company

Vantage Point Web Design is a United States company. We work remotely and serve local businesses nationwide, building and supporting your site online.
