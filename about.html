About Vantage Point

Vantage Point Web Design is a small, independent web-design studio — a DBA of Losee Productions LLC — that builds custom websites and local SEO for small local service businesses across the United States. Our whole model is built on removing risk for the owner: we design a real, custom demo of your website before you pay a cent, get you live in about 5 business days, and handle the upkeep so you never touch code.

Why we work demo-first

Most web agencies ask small businesses for a deposit before showing a single pixel — and plenty of owners have been burned by paying upfront for a site that never delivered. We built Vantage Point around the opposite promise: we do the work first, show you your real site, and only get paid if you actually want it. That keeps the risk on us, where it belongs, and keeps our work honest. If it's not good enough for you to happily pay for, we haven't earned it.

Who we serve

We focus on the businesses that need a strong web presence the most but have the least time to build one: plumbers, HVAC companies, electricians, roofers, landscapers, salons and barbershops, restaurants, and other local service businesses. We speak plain English, not tech jargon, and we handle the whole thing — design, hosting, domain and email, Google Business Profile, and local SEO — so you can stay focused on running your business.

How we work

  • You tell us about your business — no cost, no commitment.
  • We build a real custom demo of your website, free.
  • You review it live and approve the exact price before any payment.
  • We launch your site in about 5 business days and set up your Google presence.
  • You email us any change; we handle updates so you never log in.

The company

Vantage Point Web Design operates as a DBA of Losee Productions LLC, a United States company. We work remotely and serve local businesses nationwide, building and supporting your site online.
