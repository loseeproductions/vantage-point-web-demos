01 — THE PROBLEM

Most of your leads say
"Google." That is four different things.

Paid ads, your maps listing, your local services listing, and people typing your name in because a neighbour recommended you. Only one of those are you paying for. Most customer systems put all four in one bucket, and then the leads that came in by phone get nothing at all.

Why the number you need does not exist yet

Four things have to be true before anyone can tell you which dollar produced which job. On most small business setups, none of them are.

The website has to record it

A visitor arrives with the campaign attached to the link. If the site does not carry that through to the form, it is gone by the time they hit send. Most contact forms throw it away.

The phone has to be tracked

In home services most people call rather than fill in a form. One number on the website means a call from an ad and a call from a referral are indistinguishable the moment the phone rings.

The source has to survive the sale

The lead becomes a quote, the quote becomes a job, the job becomes an invoice. If the source falls off at any step, you can measure leads but never revenue.

The spend has to be joined to it

Almost no field service system stores what you spent on advertising. Without that, you can see which channel sent the most leads, but not which one was worth the money.

What that costs you, in order

These are the four things we find most often, roughly in the order they cost the most money.

  • Leads with no source at all. Usually the majority, because they arrived by phone. They cannot be backfilled later. Data that was never captured is simply gone.
  • One "Google" bucket. Paid search, local services, the maps listing and organic all collapsed together, so the ad budget is judged on a number that mostly measures your reputation.
  • A customer list nobody talks to. Most of the businesses we look at have not emailed their past customers in over a year, while referrals and repeat work are their biggest source of new jobs.
  • Quotes that just stopped. Sent, never accepted, never followed up, sitting in the system with real money attached to them.

What we actually do about it

STEP ONE

Rebuild it to capture

The site records where every lead came from, and carries it through to the form. The phone gets tracked. Booking and quote requests log a source.

STEP TWO

Audit what you have

One export tells us who has gone quiet, which quotes died, who sends referrals, and where your records are duplicated or blank.

STEP THREE

Keep it connected

Nothing stays accurate on its own. We maintain the tracking and hand you three decisions a month, each with a number attached.
