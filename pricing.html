Questions about the money

Do I have to buy all three?

No. They are sold separately and they work separately. If your site already captures properly, skip the rebuild. If you only want to know what is in your records, buy the audit and stop.

Why is the website not $500?

Because a $500 site is built to look like something. This one is built to record where your money came from, which is a different job and takes longer. If you only need a brochure, there are cheaper places and we will tell you so.

What am I locked into?

Nothing. The monthly cancels any time. Every account is in your name already, so there is nothing for us to hand back and nothing to hold.

Will you run my ads?

No, deliberately. Sending and spending on your behalf puts your compliance in our hands and raises your cost. We build the list and measure the result. You press send.
