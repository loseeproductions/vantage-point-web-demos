A route customer and a one-off repair are not the same business

A weekly cleaning customer is worth a small amount every month for years. A heater replacement is worth a lot once. Counting both as leads makes it impossible to see what your advertising is actually buying, and most pool companies are doing exactly that.

What we build into a pool service website

  • A clear split between recurring service and one-off repair work
  • Service area pages and a map so people know you run their street
  • Quote requests that record where the customer came from
  • Straightforward pricing so people stop calling just to ask
  • Google Business Profile and map-pack work for pool searches nearby
  • Review collection, because in this trade the neighbours decide

A page for each search that pays

Weekly cleaning, green pool recovery, filter and heater work and equipment repair are different searches with different value. Each deserves its own page, and each should be able to tell you whether it produced a customer who stayed.

Frequently asked questions

Can people sign up for weekly service online?

Yes. We can take the enquiry, or a full sign-up, and record where that customer came from so recurring revenue stops looking like an anonymous walk-in.

Do I need separate pages for repair work?

Yes. Repair searches and cleaning searches are different intents at very different values, and mixing them means you cannot tell which is paying.

I get most work by word of mouth. Is a site worth it?

That is the argument for it. Referrals are already your biggest source and most owners cannot name their top ten referrers. That list is worth having.
