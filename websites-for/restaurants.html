Hungry people want your menu and hours in one tap, not a PDF

When someone's deciding where to eat, they pull up your site on their phone and want three things immediately: the menu, your hours, and how to get there. A slow PDF menu or buried hours get in the way of that. We build restaurant sites that put the menu, hours, location, and a reserve/order button one tap away on any phone.

What we build into a restaurant website

  • Fast, mobile-friendly menu that's easy to read and easy for you to update
  • Hours, location, map, and directions front and center
  • Online reservation and ordering links (OpenTable, Toast, DoorDash, and more)
  • Photo gallery of your dishes and space to set the mood
  • Google Business Profile optimization for "[cuisine] near me" and "restaurants in [city]"
  • Events, specials, and catering sections if you offer them

Be easy to find at dinnertime

Most restaurant discovery now happens on Google and Maps, "tacos near me," "best brunch [city]," "restaurants open now." A polished Google Business Profile with current hours, great photos, and strong reviews, connected to a fast website, gives someone who is hungry what they need to decide. We set up both.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make my menu easy to update?

Yes. We build the menu so it's simple to change for specials, price updates, or seasonal items, no PDFs, no developer needed.

Can customers make reservations or order online?

Yes. We integrate reservation and ordering links like OpenTable, Toast, and DoorDash so customers book or order in one tap.

Will my restaurant show up on Google Maps?

That's a core focus. We optimize your Google Business Profile with current hours, photos, and reviews so you appear for "near me" food searches.

Can you add catering or private-events pages?

Yes. If you offer catering, events, or specials, we build a section for each.
