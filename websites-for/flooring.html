Flooring is chosen slowly, and measured badly

Nobody picks a floor on the first visit. They browse, they take samples home, they think about it, and they come back weeks later. By the time the sale closes, most systems have long since lost whatever brought them in, so the advertising that started it gets no credit at all.

What we build into a flooring website

  • A product library people can actually filter, by material, colour family and room
  • Room photography that shows the floor in a house, not a swatch on a screen
  • Sample requests and measure bookings that log where the customer came from
  • Showroom details, hours and directions that are easy to find on a phone
  • Google Business Profile and map-pack work for flooring searches in your area
  • Review collection, and trade credentials shown where people actually look

A page for each search that pays

People search by material and by room long before they search by brand. Carpet, hardwood, luxury vinyl, tile and natural stone each deserve their own page, and each one should be able to tell you whether it produced a measure appointment.

Flooring has one of the longest gaps between the first click and the signed order of any trade. Without something carrying the source through that gap, the spring advertising looks like it did nothing and the summer revenue looks like it appeared out of nowhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build the product catalogue too?

Yes. We can load your real product library with filters for material, colour and collection, so people can shop the way they actually browse rather than scrolling one long page.

Can you show the floors in real rooms?

Yes, and you should. A floor is easier to judge in a room than on a swatch. We use your own installation photos wherever you have them.

I have a showroom. Does a website really matter?

It matters more, not less. Most people decide which showroom to visit before they get in the car, and most systems cannot tell you which ones did.

How much does a flooring website cost?

Website rebuilds start at $1,500. A data audit of the records you already have is $1,500 to $3,000, and ongoing upkeep is $799 to $1,500 a month. Every price is published on the site.
