Booking should take three taps

Many salon and barbershop customers would rather book on their phone than call during business hours. We build salon sites where booking is the star: a big, obvious "Book now" button, a clear service menu, and a gallery that shows off your work.

What we build into a salon or barbershop website

  • 24/7 online booking front and center, synced to your schedule
  • Style/portfolio gallery and easy Instagram feed integration
  • Service menu with pricing so customers know what to expect
  • Stylist/barber profiles so clients can request their favorite
  • Click-to-call, directions, and hours for walk-in traffic
  • Google Business Profile optimization for "salon near me" and "barber [city]"

A site that looks like your shop

Your website is part of the first impression. We match the site to your shop's own look and pull in your Instagram, so new work shows up without extra effort. Online booking and your Google Business Profile are set up alongside it.
