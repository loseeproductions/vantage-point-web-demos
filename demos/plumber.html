FAQ

Questions before you call

What do I do first if a pipe bursts?

If it is safe to reach, shut off the main water valve, usually where the line enters the house or by the water meter. If water is near outlets, lights or the electrical panel, stay out of that area and call your electric utility. Then call a plumber and take photos before you clean up.

Will I know the price before work starts?

You should. Ask for the price and what it covers in writing before you say yes, and ask what could change it. If anything changes mid-job, you should be asked first.

Which cities do you serve?

This sample lists Minneapolis, St. Paul, Edina, Roseville and Bloomington. A real business sets its own service area, and says what it does after hours.

Do I need to be home during the work?

Usually an adult needs to be there to show the problem and approve the work. Tell the plumber when you book if you cannot be, so a plan can be made.

What should I have ready when I call?

Where the problem is, when it started, whether the water is shut off, and any photos you can take safely. The age of the home and of the water heater help too.

Is my drain problem in my pipes or the city sewer?

If one sink or tub is slow, the problem is usually in that branch. If several drains back up together, or the lowest drain gurgles, the main line may be involved. A camera inspection shows where. Which section is yours depends on the city and the property, so ask the plumber and your city.
