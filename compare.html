When a DIY builder is the right call

If you have time on your hands, enjoy tinkering, and mostly need a simple online business card, a builder like Wix or Squarespace can genuinely be the smart, cheap choice. The catch is that "cheap" tools still cost you many hours, and getting found on Google locally takes real know-how that the drag-and-drop editor won't teach you. If your time is worth more than the monthly savings, or ranking locally matters, DIY stops being cheaper.

When a traditional agency is worth it

If you need a large, complex, custom build — heavy e-commerce, custom web apps, a big brand system — an established agency with a $5,000+ budget is often the right fit, and we'll tell you so. What you're paying for is depth and dedicated project management. For a typical local service business that mainly needs to look professional, rank on Google, and take more calls, that budget and timeline are usually overkill.

Where Vantage Point fits

We're built for the busy local owner who wants a professional, done-for-you website and local SEO without the agency price tag or the DIY time sink. You see a free custom demo before paying anything, you're live in about 5 business days, and we handle updates and your Google presence so you never touch code. It's the middle path: agency-quality results, DIY-friendly pricing, and zero upfront risk.
