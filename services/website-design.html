THE PART NOBODY BUILDS IN

A website is where the trail starts, or where it goes cold

A visitor arrives with the campaign attached to the link they clicked. If the site does not carry that through to the form, it is gone by the time they press send. Most small business sites throw it away without anyone noticing.

Source carried to the form

The campaign, the keyword and the click id travel with the visitor across every page and arrive attached to the enquiry.

The phone treated as a lead

Most people in the trades call rather than fill in a form. Click-to-call is recorded, and the number can be tracked so an ad call and a referral call are not the same event.

Booking that logs where it came from

A quote request or appointment that cannot be traced back to what produced it is a job you can never price the advertising against.

This is what the collection layer does, and what the audit measures once it is running.
