Stop losing the customers who call after hours

Most local businesses miss a real chunk of their leads simply because they can't answer every call — the customer is booking someone at 9pm from their couch, and whoever lets them book right there wins the job. Online booking and quote forms turn your website into a 24/7 front desk that captures the appointment or the lead the moment the customer is ready.

What we can add to your site

  • Online appointment booking synced to your calendar (Cal.com, Google, and more)
  • Quote-request forms tailored to your services, sending leads straight to your inbox or phone
  • An optional AI booking agent that answers questions and books jobs automatically, 24/7
  • Instant email/text confirmations so leads don't go cold
  • Spam protection so you only get real inquiries
  • Click-to-call fallback for customers who'd rather just dial

Frequently asked questions

Which plans include online booking?

Online booking and quote requests are included on the Pro plan ($349/month) and the Growth plan. The AI booking agent is included on Growth or available as an add-on from $249/month.

Yes. We sync bookings to Cal.com, Google Calendar, and most common scheduling tools so you see appointments where you already work.

Will I get spammed with fake requests?

No. We add spam filtering and validation so you only receive genuine inquiries.

What is the AI booking agent?

It's an automated assistant on your site that answers questions, qualifies leads, and books appointments 24/7 — even after hours — then hands you the details.
