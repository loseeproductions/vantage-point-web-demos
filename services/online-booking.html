BOOKINGS WITH A PAPER TRAIL

A booking that cannot be traced is a job you cannot price

Online booking removes the phone tag. It also creates the cleanest record you will ever get of where a customer came from, but only if the booking form is wired to keep it.

The source rides along

Whatever brought them in travels through the booking and lands on the job record.

Abandoned bookings are visible

People who started and did not finish are a list worth having, and most systems never surface it.

Repeat bookings separated from new

Repeat work and referrals are the biggest source of jobs for most trades. Counting them as new leads flatters your advertising.
