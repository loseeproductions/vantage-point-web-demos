Your free Google listing is often your best salesperson

A well-built Google Business Profile shows your hours, photos, reviews, and a call button right at the top of search and Maps, before anyone even clicks a website. A neglected or unclaimed one hands those customers to whoever set theirs up properly. We claim, verify, and fully optimize yours so it works as hard as a paid ad, for free.

What we set up on your profile

  • Claim and verify your profile (or fix one that's already a mess)
  • Correct primary and secondary categories, the biggest ranking factor most owners get wrong
  • Complete services, service area, hours, attributes, and description
  • Professional photo selection and ongoing photo updates
  • Review-collection flow so you steadily earn new 5-star reviews
  • Website, booking, and click-to-call links wired directly into the profile

It works best connected to your site

A Google Business Profile and your website reinforce each other: matching name, address, and phone; matching services; and schema on your site that echoes the profile. We build both together so Google trusts the signals and ranks you higher. If you already have a site, we can optimize the profile and connect it; if you don't, it comes standard with your build.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Google Business Profile?

It's the free business listing that shows in Google Maps and local search with your hours, photos, reviews, and call button. It's separate from your website but works best connected to it.

I already have a listing, can you fix it?

Yes. We regularly take over unclaimed, duplicate, or poorly-set-up profiles, correct the categories and info, and get them ranking again.

How do I get more reviews?

We set up a simple review-request flow, usually a link or QR code, so you can ask happy customers in one tap. Steady, genuine reviews are what move the needle.

How much does it cost?

Setup and optimization are included with every website plan, or available on their own from $99/month.
