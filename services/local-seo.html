Ranking in the map pack is where local jobs come from

When someone searches "emergency plumber near me" or "best salon in [town]," the three businesses in Google's map pack get the overwhelming majority of the calls. Local SEO is the work that gets you into that pack: an optimized Google Business Profile, consistent name-address-phone across the web, real local keywords on your pages, and the schema markup that tells Google exactly where and who you serve.

What our local SEO setup covers

  • Google Business Profile optimization — categories, services, hours, service area, and photos
  • Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across your site and key directories
  • Local keyword targeting: "service + city" phrases on the pages that matter
  • LocalBusiness schema markup so search engines and AI assistants understand your business
  • Review-collection setup so happy customers actually leave you a review
  • A site structured for AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), not just classic Google

Built for AI search, not just Google

Customers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews "who's a good plumber near me?" — and those tools pull from structured, clearly-written pages. Every site we build ships with clean schema, an llms.txt file, and plain-English pages that AI assistants can quote directly. That means you show up in the new front door of local search, not just the old one.
