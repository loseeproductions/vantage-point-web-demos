Ranking in the map pack is where local jobs come from

When someone searches "emergency plumber near me" or "best salon in [town]," the three businesses in Google's map pack are the first thing they see. Local SEO is the work that gets you into that pack: an optimized Google Business Profile, consistent name-address-phone across the web, real local keywords on your pages, and the schema markup that tells Google exactly where and who you serve.

What our local SEO setup covers

  • Google Business Profile optimization, categories, services, hours, service area, and photos
  • Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across your site and key directories
  • Local keyword targeting: "service + city" phrases on the pages that matter
  • LocalBusiness schema markup so search engines and AI assistants understand your business
  • Review-request setup so asking a customer takes one tap
  • A site structured for AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), not just classic Google

Built for AI search, not just Google

Customers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews "who's a good plumber near me?", and those tools pull from structured, clearly-written pages. Every site we build ships with clean schema, an llms.txt file, and plain-English pages that AI assistants can quote directly. That gives those tools something accurate to read about you.

Ranking gets the call. It does not tell you which search paid for it

Most customer systems file paid search, Local Services, the maps listing and organic under one heading: Google. So the ad budget gets judged on a number that is mostly measuring your reputation.

Four sources, not one

Paid search, Local Services, the maps pack and organic are separated at the point of capture, so each one can be judged on what it actually returned.

Rankings tied to jobs, not clicks

A position on a page is interesting. Which searches turned into booked work is the number worth having.

Proof the work is paying

When the sources are separated you can see whether the organic work is carrying the paid work, or the other way round.

Frequently asked questions

How long does local SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile improvements can show within a few weeks; broader ranking gains build over 2 to 3 months. Nobody can deliver a #1 ranking overnight.

Is local SEO included or an add-on?

Local SEO setup and Google Business Profile setup are part of every website rebuild. Ongoing work is part of the monthly, $799 to $1,500. Every price is published on the site.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody can. Google's ranking is not for sale. We do the on-page and profile work that gives you a fair chance at the map pack.

Will AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend my business?

We structure your site so they can. We add schema, an llms.txt file, and clearly-written pages that AI search tools can read and cite.
